# Learning science and limits

Bounded evidence audit completed 2026-09-07. The sources below support learning principles; they do not validate this app, its planner, an AI evaluator, or a particular learner's outcome. This is a targeted primary-source check, not a systematic review. Implementation acceptance remains governed by ACCEPTANCE.md.

Separate modality cards prevent recognition success from standing in for spoken production; actual transfer still requires observed tasks. Exact scheduler integration belongs to domain tests.

## Evidence and content safeguards

- Record the first attempt, prior material exposure, replay, answer reveal, hints, and Chinese help. Self-reported comprehension and automated keyword coverage are estimates with different limitations. Accept meaning-preserving paraphrases; negated or unrelated keyword lists must not be treated as demonstrated understanding.
- Preserve listening, reading, retrieval, prompted production, and independent new-context production as separate evidence. Text-only performance leaves acoustic dimensions unknown. STT accuracy is not a phoneme or accent score.
- Demo difficulty is an editorial 0–1 ordering based on language and task demands, not a calibrated CEFR level. Two synthetic US-English voices provide offline practice; they do not establish natural-listening ability or accent coverage. Introduce approved real recordings and unfamiliar voices as separate transfer material.
- Shadowing is optional supplementary practice after meaning is checked: replay a short sentence, notice stress/word boundaries, record a comfortable attempt, compare A/B, then retell without reading. Focus on intelligibility and rhythm; no invented acoustic percentage or native-accent target.
- Rotating assessments retain one rubric and record the prompt/version, conditions and exposure. The three demo forms differ in language and topic and have not been psychometrically equated. Compare within conditions cautiously; use null for unobserved dimensions and never infer ability from completion alone.
- No guaranteed 30/60/90-day outcomes. The complete input → retrieval → output → repair → delayed review → novel-context transfer loop is a design informed by these sources, not itself a validated intervention.
