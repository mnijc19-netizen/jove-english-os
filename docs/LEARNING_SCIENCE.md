# Learning science and limits

Bounded evidence audit completed 2026-09-07. The sources below support learning principles; they do not validate this app, its planner, an AI evaluator, or a particular learner's outcome. This is a targeted primary-source check, not a systematic review. Implementation acceptance remains governed by ACCEPTANCE.md.

## Longitudinal and reading integration — bounded update, 2026-09-08

The original publisher pages were checked for these two 2025 syntheses. Sangers et al., [*Learning a Language Through Reading*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-025-10068-6), reports small-to-medium positive effects of extensive reading across language outcomes. Difficulty-bounded text choice and accountability were associated with larger intervention effects. The article reports that missing follow-up measurements prevented conclusions about long-term effects. This is a synthesis of intervention studies, not validation of this app's timer, word sampling or planner. Our design inference is approachable, interest-matched reading with a brief meaning response and retell, without a compulsory quiz or an achievement claim from time spent.

Alamer et al., [*Self-Determination Theory and Language Learning: A Multilevel Meta-Analysis*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-025-10038-y), reports positive associations of autonomous motivation with achievement and negative associations with anxiety. The publisher abstract was inspected; subscription full text was not accessed. These are correlational associations, not evidence that an autonomy feature causes better outcomes. Preserving interest preferences, allowing pauses and avoiding punitive backlog messages are product design choices; completing more tasks is not evidence of improved proficiency.

The implemented thresholds below are **design policies, not experimentally established optima or clinical diagnoses**:

- Recovery begins after 3/7/14 elapsed days without observed practice, initially restoring 80/65/50% of ordinary load. It increases over 3/5/7 distinct earlier UTC practice days. No overdue card is completed, deleted or rescheduled by planning; review selection is limited by estimated time, card count, FSRS decay estimates and useful error/production practice.
- A four-week performance signal requires two independent comparable practice days in every week, with explicit rubric, task family, conditions, difficulty, provenance, first pass and exposure information. Approximate flatness, a sustained 0.10 decline, or a 0.08 rise are cautious descriptive policy bands. Missing conditions stay unknown. Stable high scores are not automatically a plateau. Unknown legacy conditions are never backfilled.
- Observed imbalance uses measured session/strand seconds, not planned minutes. Possible avoidance requires explicit, resolved task opportunities and difficulty/interest-related skips across four weeks; silence, offline failures and busy days do not establish motivation or avoidance.
- Reading fit combines checked word samples, lookup attempts, personal reading-speed comparisons, editorial difficulty and a sentence-length proxy. The 95/98% vocabulary, 2/5 lookups per 100 words, relative-speed and sentence-length cutoffs are provisional heuristics. Samples from other passages cannot establish the vocabulary of an unseen text. Small or incomplete samples remain limited/unknown.
- The reading UI measures visible active time with a monotonic clock, pauses on visibility/focus interruptions and bounds long unattended intervals. Read sections are confirmed by the learner; this is not eye tracking. Optional word checking records learner-assessed recognition, not objectively established vocabulary mastery. Lookup time is excluded from reading time, while lookup attempts remain visible in observations. Time, clicks, saved responses and saved retells have **no automatic skill score**. A text retell never establishes speech or acoustics.
- AI meaning feedback is separately labelled and records its rubric and observed help/exposure conditions. Comparison requires actual provider/model provenance, not a selected Settings model that may have fallen back. Reading strata match the same editorial difficulty, length band, sentence-complexity band, rubric, segmentation/help conditions and evaluator. These are provisional descriptive comparisons, not psychometrically equated forms or standardized gains; topic familiarity and model bias can remain confounders. Four weeks still require eight distinct comparable days, so two fortnightly check-ins alone remain unknown.
- The 14-day check-in now includes a fifth, persisted reading step with a fixed approved passage, first response and retell. Unevaluated text/recording submission completes participation only and leaves reading ability unscored. Old four-step records do not acquire invented reading scores. Actual response scoring and acoustic scoring remain separate; the assessment finishes only after the reading session is genuinely saved.
- Recovery and detected patterns change selected material, available daily minutes, reading section length and spoken-task mode. A completed daily budget does not acquire zero-minute placeholders or compulsory extra reviews. Optional additional selections are explicit user choices outside the plan; all remaining FSRS cards/history stay intact. Started task identities are preserved when preferences change.
- Provider-attested scripted pronunciation/prosody may contribute reduced-weight supported acoustic practice, identified by provider and assessment ID. STT/LLM text estimates cannot enter these dimensions. Prompted/scripted speech supplies no independent speaking-fluency, interaction or transfer credit; provider results remain estimates, not a calibrated accent or phoneme diagnosis.

## Evidence and content safeguards

- Record the first attempt, prior material exposure, replay, answer reveal, hints, and Chinese help. Self-reported comprehension and automated keyword coverage are estimates with different limitations. Accept meaning-preserving paraphrases; negated or unrelated keyword lists must not be treated as demonstrated understanding.
- Preserve listening, reading, retrieval, prompted production, and independent new-context production as separate evidence. Text-only performance leaves acoustic dimensions unknown. STT accuracy is not a phoneme or accent score.
- Demo difficulty is an editorial 0–1 ordering based on language and task demands, not a calibrated CEFR level. Two synthetic US-English voices provide offline practice; they do not establish natural-listening ability or accent coverage. Introduce approved real recordings and unfamiliar voices as separate transfer material.
- Shadowing is optional supplementary practice after meaning is checked: replay a short sentence, notice stress/word boundaries, record a comfortable attempt, compare A/B, then retell without reading. Focus on intelligibility and rhythm; no invented acoustic percentage or native-accent target.
- Rotating assessments retain one rubric and record the prompt/version, conditions and exposure. The three demo forms differ in language and topic and have not been psychometrically equated. Compare within conditions cautiously; use null for unobserved dimensions and never infer ability from completion alone.
- No guaranteed 30/60/90-day outcomes. The complete input → retrieval → output → repair → delayed review → novel-context transfer loop is a design informed by these sources, not itself a validated intervention.
